# Facturación Equipo — ASSI

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** Remote specialists can move from client work to a sent, tracked invoice without losing the handoff to accounting.
- **Identity:** ASSI blue and cyan carried by the supplied mark; warm ivory and ink-blue supporting fields. Space Grotesk display with DM Sans utility copy.
- **Hero:** One invoice sheet, which becomes a share link, a payment trail, then a `.Fac` export.
- **Motion:** Snapped typographic punches, camera-like pushes through invoice geometry, and a linked cyan line that changes meaning from work to payment to export.
- **Arc:** An unfinished invoice becomes a confident end-to-end operating rhythm, then resolves into a branded lockup.
- **Payoff:** Instead of another feature panel, all process elements collapse into a single ASSI signature and concise takeaway.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Runtime:** 24 seconds.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 00.00–04.00s / 4.00s — “Del trabajo al cobro.”
**Purpose:** Establish the human problem and visual mechanism: a completed invoice carries work toward payment.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Ink-blue full bleed. A large ivory invoice occupies the right two-thirds, cropped beyond the right edge; left third holds two-line headline. A cyan line appears like a handwritten underline but then becomes the invoice's progress path. The document's geometry, not a generic dashboard, is the hero.

**Exact copy:** `DEL TRABAJO` appears at 00.15s; `AL COBRO.` punches in at 00.60s. Small line `Facturación para equipos remotos` resolves at 01.45s. Invoice header reads `FACTURA · 0248`, `Cliente: Estudio Norte`, `Total 1.240,00 €` from 01.0s through the shot.

**Choreography:** 00.00–00.45: outline invoice snaps in from a close crop with a stiff spring; cyan rule is already visible within the safe frame. 00.45–01.15: headline words appear on two hard beats while document lines print downward. 01.20–02.40: total amount resolves with a brief scale accent; cyan rule travels from invoice to headline. 02.40–03.25: the paper tilts 2° and expands toward camera. 03.25–04.00: paper edge passes across frame as a bright sheet plane; incoming shot starts with the same edge in motion.

**Transition contract:** The invoice's right vertical edge fills the frame, matching the next shot's ivory paper plane; cyan rule remains at the same y-coordinate. Compound push-through + clipped cyan band.

**Assets and technique:** Existing ASSI wordmark/mark used only in finale. Invoice built as motion-graphic artwork with CSS/SVG lines, no false product screenshot. Framer Motion scale/clip-path and layered vector rule.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental bed with sharp measured accents; the 04.00s cut lands on a measured RMS onset. No VO or SFX requested.

### Shot 02 / 04.00–08.00s / 4.00s — “Prepara. Comparte.”
**Purpose:** Show invoice preparation ending in practical client sharing.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Warm paper-white full frame and a bold cyan side-band. The invoice sheet spans most of the short edge, at a slight perspective angle; a secondary message envelope sits in foreground. Email and WhatsApp are represented as clear text labels, not trademark icons.

**Exact copy:** `PREPARA.` at 04.15s; `COMPARTE.` at 05.05s. Invoice detail `PDF LISTO` appears 05.55s. Sharing chips read `Correo` and `WhatsApp` at 06.10s and remain until exit. No CTA.

**Choreography:** 04.00–04.55: inherited sheet unrolls into invoice, with three lines writing on in sequence. 04.55–05.30: two words punch in above and below the document's fold. 05.30–06.10: a page corner lifts and folds into an envelope; cyan stroke tracks the fold. 06.10–07.10: `Correo` and `WhatsApp` labels attach to separate outgoing paths one at a time. 07.10–07.55: envelope flap rotates toward camera and its circular seal grows into the payment ring. 07.55–08.00: aperture crosses the cut with the next shot already appearing inside.

**Transition contract:** Envelope seal and cyan line become the circular status ring and payment trail in shot 3; split wipe plus matched scale aperture.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/SVG invoice sheet, fold polygon, label chips, masked type. Colors from ASSI palette. No externally hosted asset.

**Sound intent:** Music continues; invoice fold timed to a measured accent if available. No VO or SFX.

### Shot 03 / 08.00–12.00s / 4.00s — “Aceptada. Pagada.”
**Purpose:** Make acceptance and payment tracking feel observable rather than hidden in follow-up.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Return to ink-blue, but this time a thin horizontal invoice path crosses the whole frame. A circular cyan ring travels along it and leaves a sequence of bold status stamps. Large type is split across top-left and lower-right, avoiding the path.

**Exact copy:** `ACEPTADA` appears 08.20s; `PAGADA` appears 09.75s. Supporting status labels: `Enviada` at 08.10s, `Aceptada` at 09.00s, `Pago recibido` at 10.25s. Small statement `Seguimiento claro, factura a factura.` appears 10.90s.

**Choreography:** 08.00–08.60: seal expands to the status ring; cyan path draws across frame. 08.60–09.35: ring snaps to `Enviada`, then stamp `ACEPTADA` embosses into view. 09.35–10.20: ring accelerates along the path and a checkmark line traces behind it; type shifts from outline to solid. 10.20–11.20: `PAGADA` punches in as the ring reaches the final stop; supporting sentence resolves. 11.20–11.65: path bends up to gather small invoice slips. 11.65–12.00: slips rotate toward camera into a top-down sheet stack, one corner continuing into shot 4.

**Transition contract:** Final cyan ring opens as a paper aperture; scattered slips flow into shot 4's monthly statement. Push-through + split fold.

**Assets and technique:** SVG paths and stamps, CSS paper slips. No actual customer information. Statuses are generic examples.

**Sound intent:** Short musical accents on accepted / paid marks; the 08.00s cut lands on a measured RMS onset.

### Shot 04 / 12.00–16.00s / 4.00s — “Liquidaciones del mes.”

**Composition and artistic idea:** An ivory top-down desk with a deep blue statement at center. Individual invoice slips enter from multiple corners and align into rows, but only the monthly total sheet remains dominant; the aggregation is the story.

**Exact copy:** `LIQUIDACIONES` at 12.20s, `DEL MES` at 12.70s. Statement labels: `Especialista`, `Periodo · Marzo`, `Importe liquidado`. Caption `Cada especialista, su liquidación.` appears at 14.15s. Amount `1.860,00 €` is clearly illustrative, shown from 13.3s.

**Choreography:** 12.00–12.45: carried paper corner opens into a tabletop view; cyan circle traces a perimeter. 12.45–13.30: four slips arrive in a staggered cross pattern, then align into a ledger column. 13.30–14.10: statement total counts up in three restrained steps to the illustrative amount. 14.10–15.00: specialist row separates visually from the rest, emphasizing scoped records. 15.00–15.55: statement folds lengthwise into a narrow file silhouette; cyan seam stays lit. 15.55–16.00: file edge becomes the cloud/desktop boundary of shot 5.

**Transition contract:** Folded monthly statement turns into a `.Fac` file tile crossing the split boundary; masked split + perspective fold.

**Assets and technique:** Layered paper slips and ledger grid, CSS/SVG typography. No actual data or claims beyond project context.

**Sound intent:** Music's mid-film lift; the 16.00s cut lands on a measured RMS onset.

### Shot 05 / 16.00–20.00s / 4.00s — “Exporta. Importa manualmente.”
**Purpose:** Show the accurate accounting handoff without implying direct synchronization.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Split frame: cloud-blue ASSI workspace on left, neutral desktop accounting zone on right. A visible dotted gap separates systems. The `.Fac` file physically crosses the gap, while an explicit manual-import label prevents a false integration claim.

**Exact copy:** `EXPORTA .FAC` appears 16.15s; `Importación manual en Versat Sarasola` appears 17.10s. Small labels `Facturación Equipo` and `Versat Sarasola · escritorio` remain readable from 16.25s to exit. At 18.40s `Sin sincronización directa` briefly appears below the dotted gap.

**Choreography:** 16.00–16.55: folded statement opens into a file tile labeled `.Fac`; cloud-side system label enters. 16.55–17.35: dotted gap draws itself; desktop side resolves with Versat label, no logo imitation. 17.35–18.15: tile moves across gap on a straight deliberate path; `Importación manual` appears before it arrives. 18.15–19.00: the file lands and produces one expanding ring; caption about no direct sync appears briefly. 19.00–19.55: split fields fold inward around the ring, compressing to the cyan/blue curves of the supplied ASSI mark. 19.55–20.00: geometric match cut begins with mark already forming.

**Transition contract:** File's rounded-square outline becomes the frame around the ASSI mark; compound split-fold + object push-through and matching cyan geometry.

**Assets and technique:** Abstract desktop panel and file icon, no reproduction of third-party accounting software UI. Accurate manual-export wording. SVG dotted gap.

**Sound intent:** Strongest late accent around file handoff / logo emergence; the 20.00s cut lands on a measured RMS onset.

### Shot 06 / 20.00–24.00s / 4.00s — “Facturas en orden. Equipo en movimiento.”
**Purpose:** Resolve the connected workflow into a concise branded memory.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Calm warm ivory field, deliberately unlike the dark feature scenes. Official supplied ASSI wordmark is centered and large; small cyan rule anchors the baseline. Typography is above and below, not competing with mark.

**Exact copy:** `FACTURAS EN ORDEN.` appears at 20.40s; `EQUIPO EN MOVIMIENTO.` appears at 21.15s. Supplied wordmark remains fully visible from 20.65s. `Facturación Equipo` appears at 21.75s. Hold through 23.25s, then cyan rule sweeps left to right and exits as the opening invoice underline.

**Choreography:** 20.00–20.55: mark outline resolves from the previous file ring, with cyan and blue strokes converging. 20.55–21.20: official wordmark reveals by a soft mask; first line settles above. 21.20–22.00: second line and product name arrive with slower spacing. 22.00–23.25: stable, legible lockup with one subtle cyan rule sweep beneath. 23.25–24.00: rule accelerates out of frame; its right edge matches the opener's invoice edge and the dark field returns via a sharp blue/ink split so the loop starts in motion, not on an empty card.

**Transition contract:** End-to-start loop: cyan rule exits to the same y-position as the opener's line and a full-frame ink plane closes to reopen on the first invoice. No blank frame.

## Variety and continuity review
Adjacent shots change field, scale, and motion mechanic: off-centre invoice on ink, close paper fold, wide horizontal status path, top-down aggregation, split-system handoff, quiet ivory end card. Hero grows from a document to a process to an export file, then exits as the official mark. Each transition carries an explicit paper/ring/file/rule carrier. Type and cyan rule unify the film; the final card reverses the feature-scene density and preserves loop continuity.
